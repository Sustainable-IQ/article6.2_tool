The same job, done by software instead of a spreadsheet

Gold Standard and Verra consolidated every mitigation outcome authorised under either programme and shipped it as an Excel workbook so that fourteen Parties can fill in Table 4 of the annex to Decision 5/CMA.3. The data work is theirs. What follows is the same calculation, verified figure for figure against their file, in a form the spreadsheet cannot take.

Table 4, annex to Decision 5/CMA.3. Tonnes CO2eq. Shaded row numbers are your inputs; select any calculated figure to trace it.
Demonstrator. Calculation engine reconciled cell for cell against the Gold Standard and Verra workbook Article-6.2-CA-Reporting-Tool_v1.xlsx, version 1.0, 20 August 2026, data cutoff 14 August 2026. Workbook v1 mode reproduces that file exactly. Corrected mode applies the fixes listed under Assumptions and outlines every cell where the two diverge. Not an authoritative record. Parties must validate against their own records, the terms of each authorisation, and any guidance from their designated national authority.
